Free assistance to choose the best care

At CareSeekers, we know how difficult it can be for families and hospitals when a patient is ready to leave the hospital but still needs ongoing care. Finding the right support takes time, effort, and insight. That’s why our team of skilled and compassionate Care Consultants is here to assist—working swiftly to match individuals who are self-funding with suitable care homes or home care services.

We take pride in offering a warm, personalised service from start to finish. Our strong connections with care providers, hospitals, and families allow us to present a wide range of care choices quickly and efficiently. Whether it's short-term recovery support or long-term care, we ensure timely access to the most fitting care options tailored to each person's unique needs.
